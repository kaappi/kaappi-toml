## [0.1.0] - 2026-07-26

### Added
- TOML parser and serializer — `toml-read`/`toml-read-string` and
  `toml-write`/`toml-write-string`, with `toml-ref`/`toml-ref*` accessors
- Pure Scheme implementation, no C dependencies or build step
- CI workflow for automated testing
